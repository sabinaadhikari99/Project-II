{% extends "base_dashboard.html" %} {% block title %}Interview Practice | SkillSync AI{% endblock %} {% comment %} Three stages, one on screen at a time: Setup -> Interview -> Report. The page used to show setup, a question bank, a mock interview, a report, history, coaching tips and an improvement plan simultaneously - an analytics dashboard that happened to contain an interview. An interview is a linear thing, so the page is now linear too, and each stage shows only what that stage needs. What went, and why: * Question bank - a static list that also revealed the grading rubric before the candidate answered. Questions now arrive one at a time; what a strong answer covers is revealed after the answer is submitted. * Coaching tips panel - written once per session and identical on every question. Replaced by per-answer coaching from the AI Coach. * Improvement plan panel - duplicated the report's plan, gaps and courses. Merged into the report, where it is read once at the end. * Readiness sub-scores and component bars - twelve progress bars before the interview had even started. Reduced to one number and the two lists a candidate acts on: what they can lean on, and what will catch them out. Layout note (UI only): setup is a two-column workspace - the configuration form on the left, a sticky summary rail on the right holding readiness, the resumable session and the choices made so far. The interview and report stages stay in a single reading column, because a question and a report are read top to bottom. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
